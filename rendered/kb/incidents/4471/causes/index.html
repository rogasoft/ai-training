The problems

parser: day and month swap. The shared parser reads every date month first. All our tenants on file write the day first, so 7 April becomes 4 July. Where a report in this row names both dates, they are the same day and month, swapped. This is the bug #4471 was opened for. Fixed on 5 October.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type month first. Tenants on booking-v3 show MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Their people type what the hint says, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The parser fix does not touch this. Engineering retired the booking-v3 form instead of fixing the hint. Only reports sent through the form of a booking-v3 tenant are in this row; the same tenants’ app and email reports are parser reports.

one-day: the date moves by a day, it does not swap. These reports describe a date that is one day early or late, a meeting that lands on different days for people in different cities, and an invite that arrives before the booking was made. No day and month are swapped. This reads as a time zone or day-boundary bug in how invites are built. The desk notes these reports (desk-notes.json) and counts them under parser, where its view footnotes them. The parser fix will not change them.
